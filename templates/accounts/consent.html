SECTION 1 — OVERVIEW & IMPORTANT NOTICES

What This System Does

MindSpace is a mental health screening and well-being assessment system that analyses three types of data: your typed responses, voice recordings, and facial expressions. These are used to identify patterns associated with mental health conditions such as anxiety, depression, stress, and bipolar disorder.

You may choose to participate in one, two, or all three data modules. Each module is governed by a separate consent. Declining one module does not affect your decision for the others.

NOT A CLINICAL DIAGNOSTIC TOOL:
This system does not provide a medical diagnosis or treatment recommendation. It only identifies acoustic, linguistic, and visual patterns. All results must be reviewed by a qualified mental health professional before any clinical action is taken.

MODULE 1 — TEXT DATA CONSENT

Text Data Collection & Processing

This module covers your typed responses, questionnaire answers, and any written reflections submitted through the system.

What is analysed?

  • Linguistic patterns and sentence structure for cognitive state screening.
  • Emotional tone and sentiment to identify mood patterns.
  • Response content for research accuracy validation.

Technical Processing

  • Text is tokenised and pseudonymised before analysis.
  • Advanced text anonymisation techniques are applied to prevent re-identification from writing style.
  • Text data is encrypted with AES-256 during transmission and storage.

Data Retention

  • Raw text responses are retained for 6 months, then permanently deleted.
  • Anonymised linguistic features are retained for 5 years for research.

If You Withdraw

  • Text data collection stops immediately.
  • Raw text data is deleted within 7 days of your withdrawal request.
  • Anonymised data already included in published research cannot be removed.
  • Withdrawal does not affect already completed publications.

MODULE 2 — VOICE DATA CONSENT

Voice Data Collection & Processing

This module covers audio recordings of your voice during the session. The system analyses acoustic patterns, not the words you say, to identify features associated with mental health conditions.

What is analysed?

  • Prosody such as pitch, pace, rhythm, and intonation.
  • Vocal energy, spectral features, and MFCCs, also called voice texture.
  • Speech patterns associated with stress, flat affect, or elevated mood.

This is NOT:

  • Voice recognition or speaker identification.
  • Continuous background recording.
  • Storage of identifiable voice data after processing.
  • Commercial voice profiling of any kind.

Technical Processing

  • Acoustic feature vectors are extracted from your audio using OpenSMILE, ComParE 2016 feature set.
  • Raw audio files are deleted within 24 hours of feature extraction.
  • Only anonymised numerical feature vectors are retained, not your voice.
  • No voice biometric templates are created or stored at any point.

Data Retention

  • Raw audio files are deleted within 24 hours of processing.
  • Anonymised acoustic feature vectors are retained for 2 years for longitudinal analysis.

If You Withdraw

  • Microphone access is revoked immediately.
  • All voice data is deleted within 48 hours of your withdrawal request.
  • You will receive a deletion confirmation to your registered email.

Module 2 Consent Statement: I specifically consent to voice recording and analysis as described above. I acknowledge this is not voice recognition, and I understand the technical safeguards in place.

MODULE 3 — FACIAL EXPRESSION ANALYSIS CONSENT

Facial Expression Analysis

This module uses your device camera to capture facial movements in real time during the session. Only numerical scores derived from your expressions are retained.

What is analysed?

  • Micro-expressions and facial action units based on the Facial Action Coding System.
  • Valence and arousal scores derived from expression dynamics.
  • Non-verbal engagement patterns during the screening session.

This is NOT:

  • Facial recognition or biometric identification.
  • Identity tracking, profiling, or surveillance.
  • Commercial facial profiling of any kind.

Technical Safeguards

  • Raw pixel data is discarded after metric extraction.
  • Only anonymised numerical vectors are transmitted to the server.
  • Access to camera is limited to the active assessment session.

Data Retention

  • Raw video is retained only as required for processing and debugging during development.
  • Anonymised facial metric vectors are retained for model validation.
  • You may request deletion according to the data governance policy.

Module 3 Consent Statement: I specifically consent to facial expression analysis as described above. I understand this is not facial recognition.
